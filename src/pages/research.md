## Field data capture

Robust, low-cost capture of field and site data, with every record traceable back to when, where and how it was made.

## Provenance on the Internet Computer

Dataset fingerprints anchored on a public blockchain, so anyone can check that an archive hasn't changed since it was recorded.

## Universal maintenance

Hardware and software designed to be opened, repaired and interfaced with, not sealed and replaced.
